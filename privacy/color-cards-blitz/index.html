Privacy Policy for Color Cards Blitz

Last updated: 27 August 2026
Application ID: com.mobileappmentor.colorcardsblitz

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Color Cards Blitz ("the app"), an Android card game published by Prasish Sharma ("we", "us").

The short version.

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

The one exception is advertising. The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and to serve them Google receives certain device information directly. That is described in full below.

1. Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. Specifically, the app does not ask for and does not transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number or date of birth;
  • any account or sign-in credentials — there is no account system;
  • your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera;
  • your precise (GPS) location;
  • usage analytics, behavioural events or crash reports — the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDK of any kind.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally so it survives closing the game. This data never leaves your device except through the Android backup described in section 5. It is not readable by us.

What is savedWhy
Player name and character appearance you choose (up to six local players) So the table shows who is who between sessions
Coins, XP, level, unlocked cosmetics To keep your progress
Match statistics — matches and rounds played and won, cards played, challenges won, catches, streaks To fill the in-game stats screen
Settings — sound, music, haptics, reduce motion, colorblind shapes, language, table background, house rules, player count, difficulty To restore your preferences
Ad frequency counters — how many ads were shown this session and when To limit how often ads appear. Never transmitted

The name you type is a nickname of your choosing. It is never sent anywhere, and you may enter anything you like.

3. Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is the only third-party service in the app; we use no ad mediation and no other ad networks.

Where ads appear

  • a banner on the Settings screen;
  • occasional full-screen ads between matches, or when you confirm leaving a match — never during a hand;
  • optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to unlock a table background or double a match payout. These are always optional, and everything they unlock can also be obtained with coins earned by playing.

What Google receives

When an ad is requested, Google receives information directly from your device. We do not see it, and it does not pass through any system of ours. According to Google, this can include:

  • your Android advertising ID (AAID), a resettable identifier;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city or region level) location may be derived;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped.

Google acts as an independent data controller for this processing. Their handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your controls

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising on your device.
  • Change your consent choice (see section 4).

4. Consent — EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). It asks whether you agree to personalised advertising.

  • If you consent, Google may show personalised ads.
  • If you do not consent, you will still see ads, but non-personalised ones. The game is fully playable either way — no feature is withheld for declining.

You can change your answer at any time: open Settings → Ad privacy in the app, which reopens the same form. Your choice is recorded on your device by Google's SDK.

If the consent form cannot be reached — for example with no connection — the app requests non-personalised ads only. It never assumes consent it has not received.

5. Backup and device transfer

Android's own backup service may copy the app's saved data to your personal Google account, so your progress follows you to a new phone. This is a feature of the operating system and of your Google account, not of ours — we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

The ad frequency counters described in section 2 are deliberately excluded from backup.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children. Its Google Play target audience is set to 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect personal information at all.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Google through the ads in this app, please use the Google contacts linked in section 3.

7. Permissions

The app requests no permissions that prompt you. It declares:

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETRequired to load ads. Gameplay itself works with no connection.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATELets the ad SDK check whether a request is worth attempting.
AD_IDAccess to the advertising ID, used by AdMob as described in section 3.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds a small number of further normal permissions of its own. None of them display a prompt, and none grant access to your files, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

8. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on cosmetic items. Nothing in the app can be bought with real money, and nothing purchasable affects gameplay.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase. Your rights are exercised as follows:

  • Erasure of on-device data — uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Color Cards Blitz → Storage → Clear data.
  • Withdrawing advertising consent — Settings → Ad privacy in the app (section 4).
  • Data held by Google — exercise those rights directly with Google using the links in section 3.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes affecting advertising or consent will also be reflected in the app.
