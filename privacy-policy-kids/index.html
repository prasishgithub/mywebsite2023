Privacy Policy for Children's Games/Apps

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Prasish Sharma, operating under the name MobileApp Mentor (referred to as 'we', 'us', 'our'), specializes in creating games and applications ('apps') for mobile devices, web, and PC ('devices'). The terms 'you' or 'your' refer to any individuals or entities using our apps.

Protecting children's privacy

All of our apps for kids are offered without third-party advertising, analytics plugins, and in-app purchases.

For all of our kids' apps, user data and activities are only saved on the device in use and are never sent outside of this device. This includes activity and reports from the apps, which parents can download and view whenever they like.

We do include links to external activities in our kids' apps, such as rate us, view our other apps, links for worksheets, and social media page links. These are all protected inside of sections of the app only parents can access by solving a childproof puzzle.

Data collection and use

We do not collect or share any Personal Information, such as email addresses, birth dates or times, ages, names, phone numbers, addresses, etc., from users. Additionally, this webpage does not collect user data.

The only personally identifiable information we collect is when someone emails us for support. Any such personal information will never be shared with other entities. It will be retained only to resolve support requests and for future reference, such as if support is needed again. We can delete these emails at any time upon your request.

Data security

We prioritize the security of user data and ensure that all information is handled securely. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information collected through support emails only for as long as necessary to resolve support requests and for future reference. Upon your request, we will delete these emails.

User rights

Users have the right to access, update, or delete their personal data. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us at the provided contact information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, and we will post the updated Privacy Policy on this page. When we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through prominent posts on our website or within our products.
